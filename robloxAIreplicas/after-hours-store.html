AFTER-HOURS STORE

Gold squares: food · Cyan furniture: pickup range · Red figures: night patrol · White arrow: placement direction

How to survive
  • Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the touch direction pad. Your character faces the direction you last moved.
  • During the day, collect gold food packages by walking over them. Find the green REST display to recover health during daylight.
  • Press E near furniture to carry it. Press E again to place it in front of you; R rotates it. Both you and patrols collide with furniture.
  • Arrange furniture into a shelter before the lights go out. Patrols pursue you at night and damage nearby furniture when blocked.
  • Press F to eat one package. Hunger drains over time; at zero hunger you lose health.
  • Survive three nights to win. Day lasts 65 seconds; night lasts 50 seconds. Restart makes a new store layout.
  • Pausing or switching away from the tab stops the game. Progress is not saved.
