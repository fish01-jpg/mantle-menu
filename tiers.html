A note from the owner

Hey — real talk about why this page exists. I want to keep the servers free for everyone, but the servers cost money and some people talk a lot, which uses a lot of data and makes me pay a little bit to keep it all running.

So here is the deal: basic use stays completely free, and if you blow past the Free limits every day, the Pro tier is there for the heavy users. The numbers are now live, and they may still be tuned as I see how much the community actually uses.

How it works

1Buy an item

Any item at the right price works: $6.00 for Pro or $12.00 for Ultra — the buy links are in the tier cards above. The item you get is random, because only the price matters. And unless you have a 3D printer, you won't receive the physical item — your money is really paying for the tier, not the thing.

2Claim it in chat

Run /claimtier <order number> — the order number is on your Square receipt. Each order number can only be claimed once, so one purchase grants one account.

3Staff verify it

Staff check the order and set your tier, usually within a day. Your tier then stays on your account forever — daily allowances reset at midnight Pacific, your tier does not.

What tiers actually do

A tier sets your daily allowances — chat messages, server reads and writes, game fetches and profile syncs. Free covers normal chatting and gaming. Pro quadruples the messages and multiplies the rest, and Ultra removes the caps entirely.

Paid ranks also unlock perks: the supporter tag and your PRO/ULTRA tier tag (both can be equipped or unequipped from your profile), early access, a private paid-user server open the same hours as the main one, and a game pack. Ultra adds first look at Sp1der updates and one custom tag of your choice, custom made.

Fair play: free-tier daily allowances are shared per browser, so making alt accounts does not reset your free daily limit.

The numbers above are the current live limits and they may be tuned as we see how much server data the community actually uses.
