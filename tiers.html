Server Tiers

Tiers control how much server data each account can use. Every account starts on Free. Upgrade by buying an item from the Fitz's 3D Printing shop — the price you pay decides your tier (the item itself is random). Allowances reset each day; your tier stays on your account.

A note from the owner

Hey — real talk about why this page exists. I want to keep the servers free for everyone, but the servers cost money and some people talk a lot, which uses a lot of data and makes me pay a little bit to keep it all running.

So this is just a test: basic use stays completely free, and if you blow past the Free limits every day, the Pro tier is there for the heavy users. Nothing is set in stone — this is a beta, and the numbers may change as I see how it goes.

How it works (BETA)

How to upgrade: buy any item from the shop for the price of your tier — $6.00 for Pro (for example the Mini Octopus or the Dodecagonal Pyramid), or $12.00 for Ultra (the name plate at its largest size). The item is random — only the price matters. You actually get the printed item too.

Where the money goes: each purchase is split 4 equal ways — server costs, the owner and the two developers. So a $6.00 Pro purchase is $1.50 each, and a $12.00 Ultra purchase is $3.00 each.

After you buy: run /claimtier in chat (the order number is on your Square receipt). Staff verify the order and set your tier. Each order number can only be claimed once, so one purchase grants one account.

Seeing tiers: run /mytier to see your own tier and today's usage, and /tierlist to see who holds which tier. Paid tiers also show as a PRO or ULTRA chip next to your name in chat.

For staff: manage tiers with /devmanagetier (requires a staff account with a verified email and password confirmation). The older /tier @user command still works.

Fair play: free-tier daily allowances are shared per browser, so making alt accounts does not reset your free daily limit.

This page is a beta: the numbers above are the current limits and they may be tuned as we see how much server data the community actually uses.
